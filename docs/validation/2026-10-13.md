## Open failures and risks

- No independent WFP guard exists. Process-name ProxyBridge rules cannot prove
  coverage of a specific launched tree or different-name descendants.
- Upstream ProxyBridge v4.1.1 may choose DIRECT for missing PID/config; simple
  WFP outbound denial at ALE would prevent the original connection from
  reaching WinDivert's packet layer. Compatibility needs physical Windows
  experimentation and a different enforcement design.
- Real IPv4/IPv6, DNS, UDP, WebRTC, restart/crash, unrelated-app, installation
  and recovery checks were not run. Chrome, Edge, Firefox login and cookie
  continuity under this candidate were not run.
- Windows CI staged the hash-pinned installer without executing it and failed
  the acceptance gate. Its JSON artifact records the blocked state; it is not
  a leak-test result.

The active MXC Windows backend and Bubblewrap/network namespace Linux backend
were not replaced. The candidate cannot be selected for protected launch.
